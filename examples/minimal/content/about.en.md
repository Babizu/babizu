## What is this

A **made-up** language that shows off the Babizu framework. It has two dialects,
<span class="variety-badge variety-north">Northern</span> and <span class="variety-badge variety-south">Southern</span>,
plus a local variety of the south, <span class="variety-badge variety-coast">Southern (coastal)</span>.

## Correspondences

- The north says `r` where the south says `l`: `ralan` ≈ `lalan`
- Word-final `n` ≈ `ng`: `bunan` ≈ `bunang`
